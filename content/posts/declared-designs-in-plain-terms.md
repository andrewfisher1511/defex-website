If your building is Class 2 — broadly, apartments — the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 changed what has to exist before remedial work touches a building element like waterproofing or structure: a regulated design, prepared and declared by a Registered Design Practitioner, lodged before work starts.

In practice this means the era of a contractor "working it out on site" is over for this class of work. The design has to be resolved on paper first — which is precisely where remedial work goes right or wrong anyway. The Act, whatever else it did, made good practice mandatory.

For owners corporations the practical question is sequencing: engage the design before the builder, not after. A declared design lets you tender like for like, and it is the document your building keeps for the decades the repair is meant to last.

Remedial work is priced well when it is specified well. DEFEX prepares scopes of works and technical specifications that contractors can price against like for like — then runs the tender: shortlisting, clarifications, tender assessment and a written recommendation. The result is comparable prices, fewer variations, and a contract that starts from a shared understanding of the work.

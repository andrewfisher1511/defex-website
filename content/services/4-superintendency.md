title: Superintendency and contract administration
slug: superintendency
order: 4
summary: Independent AS 4000 administration — progress claims, variations and EOTs assessed on their merits through to practical completion.
---

Once works proceed, DEFEX administers the contract — typically AS 4000 — as an independent superintendent. Progress claims are assessed against work actually done, variations and extensions of time are valued on their merits, and site inspections keep the works to the declared design through to practical completion and the defects liability period.

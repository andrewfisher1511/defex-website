Where a defect becomes a dispute, DEFEX prepares reports for use in proceedings — scoped to the questions asked, confined to matters within expertise, and written as a plain statement of cause, consequence and cost of rectification. The same discipline applies whether the report supports a claim, a defence or a negotiated resolution.

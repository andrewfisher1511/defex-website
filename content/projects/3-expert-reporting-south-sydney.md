An owners corporation in dispute over balcony waterproofing and balustrade fixing defects required an independent expert assessment for use in proceedings.

Every balcony was inspected and photographed; findings were keyed to the relevant Australian Standards and NCC provisions, with a schedule of defects, causes and rectification costs prepared in a form suitable for the tribunal.

The matter resolved by negotiation, with the report's rectification scope adopted as the basis of settlement.

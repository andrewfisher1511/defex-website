A trafficked podium deck over commercial tenancies showed persistent ingress at multiple points, with previous patch repairs failing within seasons.

Targeted opening-up traced the ingress to terminations and inadequate falls rather than the membrane field. The remedial design replaced the system as a whole — falls re-established, terminations detailed, drainage upgraded — rather than continuing the cycle of patching.

A measured specification allowed like-for-like tenders, and staging kept the tenancies trading throughout the works.

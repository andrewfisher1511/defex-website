RESIDENTIAL STRATA · INNER WEST

Concrete repair Waterproofing Superintendency

Strata remediation, Inner West — 48 lots

A four-storey walk-up complex presented with spalling concrete to balcony soffits and slab edges, and recurring leaks from planters over habitable rooms.

Investigation confirmed carbonation-driven reinforcement corrosion and failed planter membranes. A remedial design and specification covered concrete repair, cathodic-risk detailing at exposed edges, and full membrane replacement with revised falls and drainage.

The works were tendered competitively and delivered under an AS 4000 contract with independent superintendency through to practical completion and defects liability.

COMMERCIAL · LOWER NORTH SHORE

Waterproofing Investigation Specification and tender

Podium waterproofing, Lower North Shore — commercial

A trafficked podium deck over commercial tenancies showed persistent ingress at multiple points, with previous patch repairs failing within seasons.

Targeted opening-up traced the ingress to terminations and inadequate falls rather than the membrane field. The remedial design replaced the system as a whole — falls re-established, terminations detailed, drainage upgraded — rather than continuing the cycle of patching.

A measured specification allowed like-for-like tenders, and staging kept the tenancies trading throughout the works.

RESIDENTIAL STRATA · SOUTH SYDNEY

Expert reporting Defect investigation

Expert reporting, South Sydney — balcony defects

An owners corporation in dispute over balcony waterproofing and balustrade fixing defects required an independent expert assessment for use in proceedings.

Every balcony was inspected and photographed; findings were keyed to the relevant Australian Standards and NCC provisions, with a schedule of defects, causes and rectification costs prepared in a form suitable for the tribunal.

The matter resolved by negotiation, with the report's rectification scope adopted as the basis of settlement.

THE PLATFORM

DEFEX — field to issued document.

DEFEX is the in-house platform this practice is built on. It exists for one reason: so that what is observed on site becomes a defensible, standards-keyed document without anything being lost or retyped in between.

DEFEX is not for sale and has no pricing page. It is how DEFEX Engineering works — and, for clients and builders, it is the login door to the projects they are connected to.

DEFEX Capture — settings
CAPTURE

Capture on site, keep the chain of evidence

Defects are recorded in the field with photographs attached at the point of observation. Originals are retained at full resolution; every image stays tied to the defect, location and inspection it evidences.

Time and activity are captured the same way — suggested from the day's real work, confirmed by the engineer, never invented.

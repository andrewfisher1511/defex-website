SERVICES

Remedial engineering, end to end.

DEFEX provides the consulting spine of a remedial project: establishing what is wrong, designing the fix, and administering the contract that delivers it. Engage one service or the full sequence.

  1. 01

    Defect investigation and diagnostics

    A defect report is only as good as its diagnosis. Investigations begin with the symptoms — water ingress, cracking, spalling, movement, failed finishes — and work back to cause through close inspection, targeted opening-up and testing where warranted. Findings are photo-evidenced, keyed to the relevant Australian Standards and NCC provisions, and reported with a clear statement of cause, consequence and recommended action. Reports are written to be read: by owners, committees, insurers and, where necessary, courts.

  2. 02

    Remedial design and declared designs

    Rectification that lasts starts with design, not with a contractor's quote. DEFEX prepares remedial designs and specifications for concrete repair, waterproofing, façade elements and associated structural work. For Class 2 buildings in NSW, regulated designs are prepared and declared under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 by a Registered Design Practitioner, ready for lodgement.

  3. 03

    Specifications and tender

    Remedial work is priced well when it is specified well. DEFEX prepares scopes of works and technical specifications that contractors can price against like for like — then runs the tender: shortlisting, clarifications, tender assessment and a written recommendation. The result is comparable prices, fewer variations, and a contract that starts from a shared understanding of the work.

  4. 04

    Superintendency and contract administration

    Once works proceed, DEFEX administers the contract — typically AS 4000 — as an independent superintendent. Progress claims are assessed against work actually done, variations and extensions of time are valued on their merits, and site inspections keep the works to the declared design through to practical completion and the defects liability period.

  5. 05

    Expert and litigation-support reporting

    Where a defect becomes a dispute, DEFEX prepares reports for use in proceedings — scoped to the questions asked, confined to matters within expertise, and written as a plain statement of cause, consequence and cost of rectification. The same discipline applies whether the report supports a claim, a defence or a negotiated resolution.

  6. 06

    Façade and waterproofing assessment

    Condition assessment before defects become claims: façade elements, balconies, planters, podium decks and roofs — falls-to-drain measured, membranes and terminations inspected, and findings reported against the relevant Australian Standards with a prioritised schedule of recommended works.
